## Intended use

Advisory detection of synthetic or cloned voices on live and recorded calls, as one layer of a
multi-layer defence. It is **not** a sole gate on any transaction (invariant I1). Out of scope:
forensic attribution of a specific person, surveillance, and any decision taken without a
human in the loop.
